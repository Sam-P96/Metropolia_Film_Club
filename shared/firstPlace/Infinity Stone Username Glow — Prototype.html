    How it works

    Layer stack (per name)
    1. Base — the full 6-colour gradient, muted, so the empty "slots" are visible.
    2. Glow — one pulsing blurred glow per owned stone (same timing as its flare) + one steady tight glow.
    3. Lit — crisp bright text on owned slices, with a thin white rim.
    4. Flares — one vertical streak + haze + core per owned stone, centred on its slice.
    5. Sheen — only when all 6 are owned.
    Your current order
    • 0 stones → plain name, no effect.
    • Order, intervals and Random are per user: your rows use your settings, other members keep theirs (KingLoanShark in the likes line uses Random).
    • Wave mode: each flare starts a little after the one to its left. With different intervals per stone they drift apart over time.
    • Random mode: every flare gets a random start and a ±30% wobble on its interval, rerolled each time the name is drawn.
